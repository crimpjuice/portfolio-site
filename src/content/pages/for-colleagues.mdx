Everything here has been used in real courses. Adapt it to your subject.

## Start with a problem

Pick one pain point. What takes you too long? What do students keep getting wrong? AI is most useful when it solves something specific.

## Available now

- **AI tutor prompts.** Full system prompts for three tutors are on the [AI Tutors](/projects/ai-tutors/) page. Swap the physics steps for your subject's reasoning steps. They work in Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, with no coding.
- **Mini-lesson prompt.** A reusable template and a live demo are on the [Intuition Building](/projects/mini-lessons-intuition/) page.
- **Lab-report feedback prompt.** On the [Smaller Tools](/projects/smaller-tools/#lab-report-feedback) page.
- **Productive Struggle rubric.** On the [How I Teach](/how-i-teach/#the-productive-struggle-grade) page.

## Principles I follow

- Start with the learning problem.
- Keep a person in the loop for judgement calls.
- Tell students what the tool is for and what it won't do.
- Expect to iterate.
