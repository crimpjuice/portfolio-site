## Getting started

If you're new to using AI in your teaching, the best first move is to identify a pain point. What takes you too long? What do students consistently struggle with? What would you do more of if it weren't so labour-intensive? AI is most useful when it's solving a specific problem.

## Principles I follow (and recommend)

- **Start with the learning problem, not the tool.** AI should solve something specific.
- **Keep humans in the loop.** AI tools should reduce friction, not replace judgment.
- **Be transparent with students.** Tell them what the tool is for, what it won't do, and how to use it well.
- **Iterate.** Your first version won't be your best version. That's fine.
