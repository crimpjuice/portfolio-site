I design learning that changes what people can do, then check whether it worked. I've spent ten years teaching college math and physics, eight years designing courses at [Brilliant.org](https://brilliant.org), and most recently AI-tutored math for young kids at Ello.

I use AI for drafting, generating and automating, so my time goes to the parts that need judgement. I also listen to my students. Some welcome AI, some distrust it, and many prefer to work through hard problems with a person. I treat all of that as design input.

## Highlights

- **Ello:** polished 31 AI-built K–2 math activities (25 live) and analysed about 89,000 learner attempts to find where kids quit. [Read the research](/projects/ello-engagement-research/)
- **Camosun:** built 6 AI learning tools in active use, and rebuilt PHYS 104 around flipped learning and productive struggle. [How I teach](/how-i-teach/)
- **Brilliant.org:** designed 30+ interactive lessons for a global platform and answered learners directly in the support queue.
- **Faculty workshops:** 4 of 5 instructors changed their course within 10 days. [Read more](/projects/faculty-workshop/)
- **Credentials:** M.TM with a thesis on cognitive load and multimedia learning, PMP, bilingual English/French.

## What I bring

- **Learning science:** cognitive load, multimedia learning, backward design.
- **AI tool design:** prompts, guardrails, and testing with real learners.
- **Measurement:** engagement analytics at Brilliant, LMS data at Camosun and drop-off analysis at Ello, with each finding rated by strength of evidence.
- **Product and support experience:** eight years working with Product, Engineering and Support at Brilliant. I tracked tickets in Linear and SQL and took bugs to Engineering with steps to reproduce them.
- **LMS:** D2L/Brightspace end to end, SCORM, LTI and interactive HTML.
