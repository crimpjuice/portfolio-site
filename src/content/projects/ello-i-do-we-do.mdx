*Ello · AI Learning Designer, Math (Contractor) · K–2 math · 2026*

## The problem

*Puddlewump Pond Fish Count* (the "I do" demonstration) and *Captain Barnacle's Submarine* (the "We do" guided practice) were meant to teach one concept. They explained it differently, which confused kids. For many children it was also their first time using this measurement tool.

## What I did

I owned the revision of both activities: the teaching flow, the AI tutor's instructions, the interactive widgets and the engagement. I worked with the math learning designer who led the activity.

- Used the same language and steps in both activities.
- Added a short first explanation and a quick review.
- Kept the story and characters, and reworked the mechanics underneath.
- Tested each flow and recorded Loom walkthroughs for expert review.

## What review taught me

- **Emotional safety beats theme fit.** Fishing hooks fit the pond story and could scare young kids. They were redesigned.
- **Every image must point at the goal.** Some visuals suggested measurement when the target skill was graphing.

## What changed

I now check each asset against the learning objective before production, and an expert sees the activity before any assets are commissioned.
