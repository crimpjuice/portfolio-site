## The problem

Instructors are busy experts. Many sit through training, agree with it, and change nothing. Both workshops were built around one question: what will you do differently on Monday?

## Workshop 1: moving online (Algonquin College, 2020)

A peer workshop for 5 STEM instructors on multimedia learning and online course design.

- Started with a needs analysis of what was stopping people from using the LMS well.
- Each instructor left with one evidence-based change for a course they were teaching.
- Measured confidence before and after, then followed up.

**Result:** confidence rose an average of 4 points out of 10. **4 of 5 instructors changed their course within 10 days.**

## Workshop 2: building lessons with AI (Camosun College)

- **Process first.** Find the concept students get stuck on, plan a mini-lesson, prompt the AI to draft it, then check and fix the result.
- **Real examples.** I showed my own mini-lessons, the prompts I used and the revisions I made.
- **Build in the room.** Everyone left with a working draft for their own course.

**Result:** **2 instructors went on to build AI-enhanced content for their own courses.** A department-wide version is in development.

## What I learned

Instructors adopt a tool once they can answer "what do I do with this on Monday?" A clear workflow answers it.
