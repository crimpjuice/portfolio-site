*Ello · AI Learning Designer, Math (Contractor) · Oct–Nov 2026*

## The problem

Only 3 of 77 math activities reached the team's 60% completion goal. Polish time went wherever someone had a hunch. Nobody could say what separated the activities kids finished from the ones they left.

## What I did

- **Data:** compared completion, session length, drop-off timing and ratings for 379 activities (about 89,000 attempts) in Mesa, Ello's authoring platform.
- **Playtests:** played the best and worst activities myself.
- **Hypothesis test:** checked my own guess about what drives engagement against the design tags Mesa stores.
- **Research:** wrote a primer on the learning science behind it (Mayer's multimedia principles, cognitive load, seductive details).

## What I found

- **Kids give a sitting about 5 minutes.** Each extra minute of math cost about 2.6 points of completion.
- **Most kids quit a few minutes in.** Leaving in the first 30 seconds was only 14% of losses. The first round or two after the hook mattered most.
- **One difficulty step can be a cliff.** In one matched pair, completion fell almost 20 points from Tier 1 to Tier 2 while ratings held steady.
- **Varied interactions help.** Each extra interaction type went with higher ratings. Packing more skills into one activity didn't help.
- **The best finisher teaches almost nothing.** *Would You Rather?* reached about 85% completion by being short, offering a choice every turn and making kids laugh. It became the benchmark for how a math activity should feel.

## How sure I was

I rated every finding by strength of evidence and named its limits. Ratings only come from kids who finish. Struggling activities get revised more often. The activities I polished finished more often (median 49% vs 43%), but without a before-and-after test I could not credit the polish, and I said so.

## What changed

The math team got a ranked fix list, a playtest plan and four design rules: **keep it short, keep kids busy, offer choices, vary the interactions.** The findings became the basis of [The Polish Playbook](/projects/ello-polish-playbook/).
